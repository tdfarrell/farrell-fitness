Farrell Fitness

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Farrell Fitness is a personal nutrition, training and health tracker run by Tim Farrell at farrellfitness.com. It is a private app used only by its owner and anyone the owner gives an account to. It is not sold, advertised or offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not collect your location or contacts. The camera is used only while you scan a barcode; images are read on your device and are never uploaded or stored.

Health records and journal entries are sensitive. They are stored with the rest of your data, shown only to you, and included when you download your data.

How WHOOP data is used

When you choose to connect WHOOP, you sign in on WHOOP's own website and approve read-only access. The app then reads your recovery, sleep, cycle (strain), workout, profile and body measurement data through the WHOOP Developer API. That data is used only to show you your own recovery, sleep, strain, steps and workouts inside the app, alongside your nutrition, training and health records, and to check off habits such as a daily step goal.

  • WHOOP data is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • It is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling.
  • It is never used to train machine-learning or AI models.
  • The app only reads data. It never writes to or changes your WHOOP account.

Where data is stored

All data is stored in a private MySQL database on the app's web host (Bluehost) in the United States. WHOOP access tokens are stored on the server and are never sent to your browser. Some data is also kept in your browser's storage so the app works offline; it stays on your device.

Who data is shared with

Your data is not shared with third parties. To provide its features, the app sends only the following:

  • USDA FoodData Central receives the food search terms you type, to return nutrition information.
  • Open Food Facts receives a product barcode when you scan or enter one.
  • WHOOP receives requests for your own data, signed with the access you granted.

None of these requests include your name, your logs, your health records or your WHOOP data.

Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
  • Sign-in cookies are HTTP-only. The "stay signed in" token is stored hashed and replaced each time it is used.
  • The server configuration and database are not reachable from the web.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual entries at any time in the app, including medications, lab results, blood sugar readings, habits and journal entries, and download everything you have stored from Settings.

To stop WHOOP access, go to Settings and choose Disconnect WHOOP. This revokes the app's access with WHOOP and deletes the stored access tokens and all WHOOP data the app has synced. You can also remove the app's access from your WHOOP account settings at any time. To have your account and all of its data deleted, contact the owner below.
